What is in this booklet
  • 29 chapters that cover the grammar you need for upper secondary English: verbs, nouns, articles, pronouns, adjectives, prepositions, linking words, punctuation and spelling.
  • Common mistakes in every chapter, especially mistakes made by Swedish and Arabic speakers.
  • Over 200 exercises with a full answer key, and three texts with hidden mistakes.
  • A proofreading method so that you notice errors in your own writing.

Written in simple English. The rules and examples follow standard British and international English grammar. The books and websites in the Sources chapter at the end (Swan, Murphy, Cambridge, British Council and others) can be used to check any rule.

★How to use this booklet

The 4-round method
  1. Round 1: Read. Read one chapter slowly. Say the example sentences aloud.
  2. Round 2: Cover and say. Cover the rule boxes with a sheet of paper. Try to explain the rule in your own words. Check.
  3. Round 3: Exercises. Do the exercises for that chapter (Part 6). Check with the answer key. Write every mistake in a list: rule + correct sentence.
  4. Round 4: Repeat after 2 days and after 7 days. Do only the exercises you got wrong. This is how the rules move to your long-term memory.
A plan that works

20–30 minutes a day is better than 3 hours once a week. Study one or two chapters per day. Then read the Proofreading chapter (29) and use it on every text you write. When you can find mistakes in the three hidden-mistake texts at the end, you are ready.
