★Quick reference: one page to keep

Tense formulas

Present simple: V1 (+s)
Present cont.: am/is/are + V-ing
Past simple: V2 · Past cont.: was/were + V-ing
Present perfect: have/has + V3
Past perfect: had + V3
Future: will + V1 · am/is/are going to + V1
Passive: be + V3
Modal: modal + V1 · modal + have + V3

Conditionals

0: if + present, present
1: if + present, will + V1
2: if + past, would + V1
3: if + had + V3, would have + V3
wish + past (now) · wish + had + V3 (past)

Time words

for + period · since + starting point
ago + past simple · already/yet/just + perfect
when/while/before/after/if/unless + present for the future

Prepositions of time and place

at 5, at night · on Monday, on 5 May · in May, in 2024, in the morning
in a city · on a street · at a number/point

Articles

a/an = one, first mention · the = specific, known · nothing = plural/uncountable in general, names, languages, sports, meals

Contrast

although + clause · despite + noun/-ing · however, + new sentence · whereas = compare

The 10 most common mistakes

1 he/she/it + -s · 2 a/the missing · 3 informations/advices · 4 tense (perfect vs past) · 5 depend of / good in · 6 word order in questions · 7 will after when/if · 8 its/it's, their/there · 9 comma splice · 10 capital letters (I, Monday, English)

★Sources and how to use them

Honest note: this booklet was written from the author's knowledge of English grammar, and is organised after common grammar syllabi. It was not copied from any single book. Grammar rules are stable, but you should check anything that feels unclear against the reliable sources below. If a source disagrees with this booklet, trust the source and correct your copy.

SourceWhat it is good for
Michael Swan, Practical English Usage (Oxford University Press)The standard reference for "why is it said this way?". Look up any word or rule in the index.
Raymond Murphy, English Grammar in Use (Cambridge University Press)Clear explanations with many exercises, ordered by topic.
Cambridge Dictionary: Grammar (dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar)Free, searchable grammar explanations with examples (British English).
British Council, LearnEnglish Grammar (learnenglish.britishcouncil.org)Free grammar lessons and exercises with answers, level-marked A1–C2.
Purdue OWL (owl.purdue.edu)Free writing and punctuation guide, essays, proofreading.
Oxford Learner's Dictionaries (oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com)Definitions, pronunciation, grammar patterns for each word, irregular verbs.
Collins COBUILD / Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English (ldoceonline.com)Authentic example sentences; usage notes; common collocations.
Your school's English teacher and textbookAlways the final authority for your grade: ask them if you are not sure.
